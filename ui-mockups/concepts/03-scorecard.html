First 100 days

Clear priorities. Early momentum.

Planned

Listen and learn

Meet members across the association. Gather their priorities, understand current initiatives and identify where REAN can make the greatest difference.

  • Structured member conversations
  • Review current programs and partnerships
  • Share a summary of key themes
Planned

Align around priorities

Work with members and the association’s leadership to turn what we hear into a focused, shared agenda.

  • Agree near-term priorities and measures
  • Clarify member engagement pathways
  • Share an initial delivery roadmap
Planned

Begin delivery and report back

Start the agreed work, make progress visible and create a dependable rhythm for member updates.

  • Launch priority working groups
  • Report early progress and open questions
  • Set the next review point with members

Proposed framework. Actions and timelines to be confirmed with members and REAN governance.
